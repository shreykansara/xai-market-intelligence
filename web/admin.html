Full Pipeline Orchestrator & Auto-Purge Manager

Execute end-to-end multi-stage pipeline runs. The orchestrator transitions data seamlessly from Stage 1 to Stage 2 and into Stage 3, enforcing automatic purging and deduplication at every step.

Stage 1: Ingest Raw GDELT News
Streams archives and stores records in raw_gdelt_news with ID/URL deduplication.
Stage 2: Basic NLP Filter & Discard Noise
Extracts business headlines, categorizes across 11 dimensions, moves to Stage 2, and deletes processed rows from Stage 1.
Stage 3: 768-D Embeddings to 11-D Model Projection
Generates embeddings, applies linear matrix model to 11 dimensions, ingests into master DB, and deletes promoted rows from Stage 2.
